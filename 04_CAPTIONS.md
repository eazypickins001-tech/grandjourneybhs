# 04 — CAPTIONS, HASHTAGS & PINNED COMMENTS

> Filled once for this project. Every post reuses the structure and only the
> hook line and body change.

---

## 1. Caption rules

1. **The first line is the hook.** Same punch as the on-screen text, rewritten
   for the platform. Never start with context.
2. **Short lines.** One idea per line. Line breaks are pacing.
3. **No hashtags in the middle of a sentence.** Either end of caption or first
   comment — pick one and stay consistent. This project: hashtags at the end.
4. **The link goes in the bio on Instagram/TikTok**, and in the caption on
   YouTube/Facebook. The phone number is the primary destination.
5. **One CTA.** Not "follow, like, share and comment".
6. **The caption must still work if the video never plays.**
7. **Every caption ends with the two fixed lines** (see §7): the education
   disclaimer and the crisis line. This is not optional in healthcare.

## 3. Hashtag strategy

Use **8–12** per post, three tiers, every time.

| Tier | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach** (high volume) | Discovery | `#mentalhealth` `#mentalhealthawareness` `#therapy` `#recovery` `#selfcare` |
| **Creator / audience** (the people we want) | Local clients, families, referrers | `#baltimoretherapist` `#counseling` `#behavioralhealth` `#substanceuserecovery` `#psychiatry` |
| **Niche** (low competition, on-brand) | Local intent + services | `#baltimore` `#baltimoremd` `#maryland` `#medicaid` `#psychiatricrehabilitation` |

Rules:

- Keep the same core set so the algorithm learns the topic.
- Rotate 2–3 reach tags per post to test.
- Never use a hashtag the content does not earn (no `#depression` on a careers
  post; no `#recovery` on a Medicaid-admin post).
- Do not use diagnosis hashtags for engagement (`#bipolardisorder` is a topic,
  not bait).

**Project hashtag set:**

```
REACH:   #mentalhealth #mentalhealthawareness #therapy #recovery #selfcare
CREATOR: #baltimoretherapist #counseling #behavioralhealth #substanceuserecovery #psychiatry
NICHE:   #baltimore #baltimoremd #maryland #medicaid #psychiatricrehabilitation
BRAND:   #grandjourneybhs #grandjourney
```

## 4. Pinned comments

Every post gets one pinned comment, written before publishing. Its job is to
start the thread, not to summarise the post.

Patterns for this project:

- **The usefulness:** "If you are getting ready for a first appointment, the only
  things you need are your ID, your insurance card and this phone number:
  833-888-0805."
- **The stake:** "The hardest part is usually the first call. If you have been
  putting it off, this is the week."
- **The invitation:** "What was the one thing nobody told you before your first
  session?"
- **The safety:** "If you or someone you love is in crisis right now, please call
  or text 988 — do not wait for an appointment."

**Never pin** "Thanks for watching", a diagnosis, or anything that confirms
someone in the comments is a patient.

## 5. Comment seeding (three per post)

Each post ships with three comments in `03_POST_BANK.md`. They must be:

1. **Specific** — about this post, not any post.
2. **Answerable** — a viewer can reply in five words.
3. **Non-promotional** — they invite, they do not sell.

Weak → strong:

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Great video!" | "Which part of intake surprised you most?" |
| "Thoughts?" | "What did you wish you had known before your first session?" |
| "Follow for more" | "Which step would you want explained next — PRP or medication?" |

---

## 6. Publishing rhythm

- Match cadence to `00_DNA.md` (1 story + 4 shorts/week; launch at 3/week).
- Strongest post of the week goes out first.
- Stagger shorts from the same research across the week.
- Never two salesy posts back to back.
- Post, then pin the comment immediately.
- Reply to every comment for the first 90 days — as a human, from the clinic.
- **Community rule:** never confirm or deny patient status in a comment or DM;
  move the conversation to 833-888-0805.
