## 1. Pillars

| # | Pillar | What it covers | Tone | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What Treatment Actually Looks Like** | PRP, individual/group/family therapy, psychiatric evaluation, medication management, IOP/PHP, substance use treatment — what happens, who is in the room, how long it takes | Plain, reassuring, concrete | 30% |
| 2 | **Myths & Reality** | Stigma: "therapy is for weak people", "meds change who you are", "just pray it away", "recovery is willpower" | Direct, corrective, never mocking | 20% |
| 3 | **For Families & Caregivers** | Warning signs, how to talk to a loved one, boundaries, what to do when they refuse help, caring for the carer | Gentle, practical, non-judgmental | 20% |
| 4 | **Getting In The Door** | Maryland Medicaid, insurance, referrals (school, doctor, court), intake, what to bring, telehealth, waitlists, cost | Practical, local, step-by-step | 20% |
| 5 | **The People & Place** | Clinicians and staff, careers, CARF accreditation, the Virtual Calming Room, community | Warm, human, proud without hype | 10% |

Rules:

- Every post belongs to exactly one pillar.
- Never two consecutive posts in the same pillar (unless the sub-type differs).
- The share column is a target, not a quota — the standing instruction wins.

## 3. Rotation rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| No two consecutive posts in the same INTERRUPT category | The audience adapts to a repeated trick within days |
| No two consecutive posts in the same pillar | Prevents the feed reading as "one topic" |
| At least 4 different categories per week | Keeps the catalogue visually varied |
| Never two salesy posts in a row | Protects trust — critical for a care provider |
| One proof post per ~5 posts | Proof earns the right to ask for the call |
| Every symptom-adjacent post carries the crisis line and disclaimer | Safety is not a creative choice |

## 5. Evergreen series

| Series | Format contract | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **First Session** | Third-to-first-person walk-through of one intake milestone; object-led; ends on "that is the whole step" | 1 per 2 weeks |
| **Myth Monday** | One myth stated plainly, held for 2 s, then corrected with a mechanism; never mocks the believer | weekly |
| **The Empty Chair** | Caregiver arc told through a single object; no faces; ends on a boundary or an invitation | 1 per 2 weeks |
| **From the Front Desk** | One access fact per post, with the exact step and the phone number | weekly |
| **The Calming Room** | 20–30 s paced breathing or grounding; minimal narration; loopable | as needed |
